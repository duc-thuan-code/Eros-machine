About

First screen shows a pure black background with your logo.png. After you scroll, the background rotates through BG1.jpg, BG2.jpg, BG3.jpg every 3 minutes. Up to 12 projects supported. Each project uses a cover Pxx-0.png plus up to five additional images (Pxx-1.png … Pxx-5.png).
