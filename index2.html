About Yellow Pages

Yellow Pages publishes books on—naturally—yellow paper. The project was initiated by Olivier Lebrun and Urs Lehni. It embraces black & yellow as a signature palette (with room for other colors too) and is stocked by international distributors.

This is a single‑file front‑end recreation for study/demo use. Swap in your own copy, links, and pricing as needed.
